# 利益冲突声明 (Conflict of Interest / Competing Interests)

## 无利益冲突

---

**Option 1 (推荐):**
The authors declare that they have no known competing financial interests or personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

**Option 2:**
The authors declare no conflict of interest.

**Option 3:**
The authors have no competing interests to declare that are relevant to the content of this article.

---

## 有利益冲突

如果存在潜在利益冲突，必须如实披露：

---

**示例 1 - 资金相关:**
[Author Name] has received research grants from [Company Name]. The remaining authors declare no conflict of interest.

**示例 2 - 咨询关系:**
[Author Name] serves as a consultant for [Company Name]. [Author Name] has received speaker fees from [Company Name]. The other authors declare no competing interests.

**示例 3 - 股权:**
[Author Name] holds equity in [Company Name], which develops products related to the research described in this paper.

---

## 需要披露的利益冲突类型

- **财务利益**: 资金、股权、专利、咨询费、演讲费
- **非财务利益**: 董事会成员、志愿者角色、专家证人
- **关系**: 与可能受研究影响的公司或组织的关系

## 时间范围

通常需要披露过去 3 年内的利益冲突（具体以期刊要求为准）。

## 注意事项

- 诚实披露不会导致论文被拒
- 隐瞒利益冲突是严重的学术不端行为
- 如有疑问，宁可披露
